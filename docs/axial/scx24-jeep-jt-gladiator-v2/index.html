01 /

The long pickup is the buying case.

The green V2 Gladiator is an appealing first micro crawler when scale pickup detail and a longer chassis are the point of the purchase. Its current published wheelbase is 155mm, compared with the 4Runner's 132mm. That difference is large enough to matter when planning the obstacle course and ordering links or driveshafts. It is not evidence that one is universally more stable. I would choose the Jeep for the overlanding pickup presentation and a course that lets its longer proportions make sense.

02 /

V2 means Spektrum equipment and oil-filled shocks.

This exact AXI00005V2T3 includes a Spektrum SLT2 radio, 350mAh 2S LiPo, USB-C charger and four AA cells. The revised oil-filled shocks are also part of the current official description. Earlier Gladiator packages used a different control arrangement, so an old four-channel radio description is not the current inventory. Inspect the box against the V2 list and keep the manual revision with the truck. An attractive used Gladiator can be a different electronics generation even if the body is familiar.

03 /

Worm axles support a slow-crawling purchase.

The 030 brushed motor drives front and rear worm-gear axles through the compact SCX24 transmission. Worm reduction contributes to low gearing and descent behavior, but it does not make the vehicle immune to getting stuck or slipping sideways. When the tires are wedged against an obstacle, release the load and choose another line rather than holding the trigger indefinitely. The factory drive arrangement makes sense for technical micro crawling, while it gives little reason to buy this Jeep for covering a long trail at walking speed.

04 /

The current radio should be judged on its own terms.

SLT2 proportional controls and reduced throttle settings define the V2 control package. The historical Small-Scale RC review praised the original Gladiator's radio and response, but that equipment differs and its observations cannot establish V2 behavior. Follow the V2 manual for binding and controller setup. A useful first exercise is placing one front tire at an obstacle edge with a small trigger input, keeping the same limiter setting while learning.

05 /

Long wheelbase requires matching parts, not a generic SCX24 order.

A three-link front and four-link rear suspension support the Jeep's solid axles, with oil damping in the current version. The long chassis changes the relevant link and driveshaft combinations. Parts labeled SCX24 can be intended for a different wheelbase, which is why the Gladiator drawing matters. The current product table uses a rounded 155mm wheelbase while parts descriptions use 153.7mm. We preserve that difference and do not present it as a home measurement or a reason to force a mismatched part into position.

06 /

Give the middle of the truck room to clear the crest.

The Nitto Trail Grappler M/T tires and KMC Machete wheels give the stock Jeep its scale identity. The longer axle spacing can be helpful for some obstacle shapes and awkward when the center bridges a sharp crest. That is a geometry consideration, not a tested climbing score. Build gradual approach and exit sections at home and leave a way to reverse out. Axial's current table says the vehicle is not water resistant, so choose dry practice conditions rather than treating a trail photograph as waterproof permission.

Scale accessories add interest and inspection points.

The Gladiator uses a detailed polycarbonate body with overland accessories, an interior treatment and bumper LEDs. Their visual appeal can justify choosing the pickup, but decorative items are not evidence of carrying capacity or a measured durability advantage.

BEFORE YOU ORDER /

The questions worth asking.

What changes with the Gladiator V2 package?+

The reviewed green AXI00005V2T3 lists Spektrum SLT2 controls and oil-filled shocks. Historical original-Gladiator radio observations are not evidence for those V2 components.

What power equipment comes with it?+

The documented V2 box includes a 350mAh 2S LiPo, USB-C charger and four AA transmitter cells. Use its own charging and setup instructions.

Is the wheelbase 155mm or 153.7mm?+

The current product table rounds it to 155mm, while parts descriptions use 153.7mm. Use the exact Gladiator parts drawing when matching links and driveshafts instead of assuming every SCX24 part fits.

Can I use it on wet trails?+

The recorded official table says the vehicle is not water resistant. This review recommends dry practice conditions rather than inferring waterproof capability from the Jeep body or trail photography.

Why compare the Toyota 4Runner?+

The 4Runner is another complete SCX24, with a shorter 132mm wheelbase and an ABS SUV body. The Gladiator’s long polycarbonate pickup and accessories suit a different scale preference and obstacle geometry.

Has its longer-wheelbase stability been tested here?+

No. The review explains the geometry tradeoff and documented package. It does not measure stability, climbing, runtime or durability, and the editorial score is not a drive-test result.
