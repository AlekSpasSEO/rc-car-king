02 /

A complete box still needs a first-session plan.

For this original package, the manual identifies the assembled truck, SLT2 radio, AS-1 steering servo, combined ESC/receiver, 350mAh 2S battery, USB charger and four AA cells. Those are the expensive decisions a new owner might otherwise need to make separately. The important benefit is avoiding a compatibility shopping exercise before learning whether crawling is enjoyable. There is no reason to order another drive pack or a premium charger as a condition of trying the stock vehicle.

The remaining practical requirement is a suitable USB power supply. The model manual specifies a 5V source with at least 1A output for the supplied charger. Use its instructions and indicator chart, supervise charging and disconnect the pack when the cycle finishes. A supplied charger is part of the package, not permission to ignore the charging routine. Before fitting the battery, look for cable damage and make sure the connector and leads will not be trapped beneath the body.

Separate the first drive from the first decorating session. Identify the accessory bag, retain the screws and start with the truck in its simpler configuration. The optional rack can wait until you have a reference for how your course works. On an online purchase, check those contents immediately against the manual and seller description. A photographed radio beside a truck is less useful than confirming that the actual box includes the matching charger, battery and small fittings.

03 /

Reduction changes the job of the motor.

The documented motor is a brushed Dynamite 88-turn 030 unit, part DYNS1217. The drivetrain uses shafts to carry drive to both axles, where worm gearing adds reduction. This is a system intended to place the tires carefully. Motor size, turn count and the word 4WD cannot tell you a measured climbing angle, and we have not obtained one. What they do tell the buyer is that this is a stock crawling package rather than a miniature high-speed truck.

A worm drive puts a screw-shaped gear against its mating gear. In general, this creates substantial reduction and resistance to being driven backward by the wheels; it helps explain why these axles feel different from a freely rolling road-car transmission. That mechanism is useful context for setup, rather than a promise that this particular truck will hold every slope. Tire contact, the course angle and the body’s position still decide whether the vehicle has a workable route.

Treat a stalled wheel as a question about the line before treating it as an invitation to buy more motor. A tire wedged between rocks can load the drivetrain without moving the truck. Back out, approach from another angle and check for fouling. If the motor or controller becomes hot, stop and let the equipment cool as the manual directs. More throttle applied to a trapped truck offers poor information about the stock system and can turn a simple obstacle into a repair.

04 /

Use the travel adjustments before changing hardware.

The SLT2’s documented throttle limit has 50, 75 and 100 percent positions. Start with the lower setting while checking a small course, then adjust if the available control range suits you better elsewhere. This limits the maximum command; it does not convert the truck to another gear ratio or add traction. A useful early exercise is driving slowly between two markers with enough room to stop, reverse and re-center the steering without contacting either one.

The steering-rate control and programmable servo travel address another ordinary setup problem: demanding more steering than the mechanism can use. Check both directions with the wheels clear of the ground, and use the manufacturer procedure for travel settings. A servo buzzing against a mechanical stop should prompt inspection and adjustment. Do not assume the largest possible radio setting is the best one. Learn the stock turning space on a clear surface before building a tight corridor that requires repeated correction.

The model manual’s receiver diagram distinguishes forward/reverse mode, recommended for crawling, from forward/brake/reverse mode. Consult that diagram before changing the jumper, and check the result with the wheels raised. Its parts table calls the SLT2 a three-channel transmitter, while the product description says two-channel; the separate ESC instructions associate auxiliary CH3 use with an SLT3. We therefore describe the fitted SLT2 controls and do not promise a working stock third channel, remote gearshift or added accessories from that conflicting label.

05 /

A scale body gives the suspension another job.

Oil damping resists rapid shock movement, while a spring supports the vehicle. Those are different functions. Preload adjustments can change the starting position, but they do not magically create grip or remove body weight. For a beginner, the useful baseline is consistent assembly: check that the suspension moves without a cable, accessory or wheel rubbing where it should not. Change one setting at a time so that any difference on the same obstacle has an identifiable cause.

The four-door hard body is part of the Power Wagon’s reason to exist. The general tradeoff is that weight positioned higher in a vehicle can reduce the margin before tipping on a side slope. We have not measured this model’s center of gravity or rollover angle. I would begin without the upper rack, use low side-slope obstacles and record which approach fails. That gives you a reason for a later adjustment instead of copying a brass-parts shopping list without knowing what problem it solves.

Wheelbase also changes the route you choose. A longer distance between axles can bridge some gaps, but a sharp crest can leave the center section resting on the obstacle before the rear tires climb it. The product page lists 155mm, and the chassis/link parts are named 153.7mm in the manual. Keep both figures visible rather than claim a precision the documents do not share. On a homemade course, a shallow transition teaches more than a sudden step sized to catch the skid every time.

06 /

Make the obstacle smaller before making the truck heavier.

The supplied Pro-Line Super Swamper tires and beadlock wheels give this package a clear equipment identity. A tread pattern can present edges to a surface, but the route, surface texture and how the tire is loaded matter as well. We have not tested grip or compared compounds. Build a first obstacle with an easy approach and a clear exit. If it becomes possible only after repeated full-throttle attempts, reduce the height and practice placing each front tire instead.

Choose dry, stable objects for an indoor course and keep the driving area clear of an unprotected table edge. Outdoors, think in terms of individual small rocks, short twigs and bare patches rather than a miniature version of a full-size trail. Tall grass can surround small tires and hide the intended line. A long drive across an unsuitable yard is unlikely to reveal why this category is interesting. Taking the truck to a suitable patch and working a short route is a better use of it.

Axial labels the vehicle water-resistant. We have no immersion test and would not make water part of the buying case. Dirt and dampness also increase the cleaning work around small mechanisms. RC Driver’s September 2023 launch coverage highlights the lights, scale accessories and suspension equipment; it is useful corroboration of the package, not a source of quantified handling results. Its enthusiasm does not replace the need to match the truck to your own obstacles or make the water-resistance label broader.

07 /

Keep the nice body, and make routine access easy.

The green body is a custom four-door interpretation of a 1940s Dodge Power Wagon. A buyer who enjoys looking at that cab and bed gets something different from a minimally bodied competition project. RC Driver identifies working lights and a functional tailgate in its model coverage. The official photographs also let you distinguish the unadorned truck from the accessory-filled presentation. Decide whether that scale character belongs in your garage before treating the body as something that must immediately be replaced.

Routine access should stay routine. Follow the body-removal instructions, watch the light wiring and keep the battery leads clear when closing the vehicle. Small screws and plastic mounts reward a measured hand: use the appropriate driver, keep removed hardware together and avoid tightening by force after a fastener seats. The manual’s exploded drawings help identify an assembly before ordering a replacement. That is more useful than assuming every part labeled SCX24 is a direct fit for this long-wheelbase version.

Ownership planning starts with the exact version rather than the SCX24 family name. Check whether your chosen retailer can supply the model-specific body mounts, stock electronics and drivetrain items you expect to need. We have not surveyed local stock or counted failures, so the documentation score is about available instructions rather than guaranteed parts supply. After a session, remove debris, inspect visible wiring and disconnect the battery; let the observed condition determine maintenance instead of assigning an invented replacement schedule.

BEFORE YOU ORDER /

The questions worth asking.

Is the linked car an FMS Power Wagon or an Axial replacement body?+

Neither. It is the complete green Axial SCX24 original RTR, AXI00007T2, ASIN B0CL8VMLND. Amazon Creators API returned brand Axial and that exact manufacturer PartNumber on 14 October 2026.

What do I need beyond the box?+

The documented original package includes the drive battery, USB charger, radio and four AA cells. You need a suitable USB supply: the model manual specifies 5V and at least 1A. An optional spare battery is not required to start.

Does the reference figure guarantee the Amazon price?+

No. Axial listed the exact green complete RTR at $159.99 on 14 October 2026. Amazon pricing is checked separately through its API and expires after 24 hours. Confirm the retailer’s current delivered price, exact package and purchase terms.

Which width and height are correct?+

The product table lists 104mm width and 107mm height; manual page 3 lists 107mm width and 100mm height. We have not measured the truck and cannot resolve the conflict. Leave room beyond either figure when planning storage or a course.

Can the stock radio operate a third accessory channel?+

Do not assume so from the manual’s three-channel table entry. The product describes SLT2 as two-channel, and the separate ESC manual associates CH3 with SLT3 use. Verify the fitted radio and receiver instructions before planning an auxiliary accessory.

Should I install the rack or buy weight upgrades immediately?+

Our buying advice is to start with the simpler body configuration and learn a repeatable course. Weight high on a vehicle can affect balance, but we have no measured rollover angle or proof that a particular upgrade is required.

Is 7.8/10 a customer rating or a physical test result?+

No. It is our research-led buying assessment for a beginner scale-crawling buyer, calculated from five visible category scores. We have not measured this truck’s speed, runtime, durability, grip or reliability.
