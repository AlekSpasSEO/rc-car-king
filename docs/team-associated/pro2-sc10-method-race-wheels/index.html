The rear differential and slipper do different jobs.

The Reedy 3300kV brushless system drives a rear gear differential through the 2.6:1 gearbox. The externally adjustable V2 slipper provides a separate means of managing drivetrain load, while the differential permits different rear wheel speeds. Treating both adjustments as one traction knob hides their different roles. Start with the manual's factory settings and a suitable initial power level. If a driving problem appears, inspect gear mesh and free drivetrain movement before buying a stronger motor or tightening every adjustment in the hope of improving it.

The documented Reedy 3300kV brushless motor; SC600BL ESC is the power equipment evaluated for Pro2 SC10 Method Race Wheels. Start from the original 70021 gearing and battery specification. Keep optional electronics purchases separate until the stock configuration reveals a specific requirement worth changing.
