02 /

Ready-to-run assembly still leaves a battery-selection job.

The current manufacturer contents include the assembled truck, finished clipless body, 3100Kv motor, 80A two-in-one ESC/receiver, SLT3 transmitter, S662 metal-geared servo, AA transmitter cells and basic tools. The drive electronics are fitted, so you do not need to install a power system or paint a shell first. That makes this a complete vehicle purchase rather than a roller or chassis project. It still omits the rechargeable equipment that supplies its ordinary driving sessions.

ARRMA requires a suitable 2S or 3S hardcase LiPo and charger. Choose the exact connector and physical fit as well as the cell count; the current specification identifies IC5. A charger intended for a different chemistry does not complete the basket. If its input adapter or cable is separate, count that too. You already receive transmitter AAs in this particular package, so do not accidentally add a compulsory extra row for batteries supplied by the manufacturer.

For the initial bench check, inspect the restraint, lead routing, wheel security and body seating. Check steering direction and travel using the manual, then set a conservative control configuration before placing the truck on the ground. An RTR removes assembly decisions but leaves setup decisions. A first brushless buyer benefits from a known baseline: stock tires, factory gearing, a properly fitted pack and a simple route. Leave optional parts aside until you have seen the vehicle work normally.

03 /

The current center differential is a generation-specific feature.

The 3100Kv brushless motor feeds a shaft-driven 4WD system with an all-metal-geared center differential in the documented current module. That center unit permits a relationship between front and rear drive speeds rather than behaving like the fixed coupling or slipper arrangement you may remember from another Granite. It is a substantive reason to read the exact V4 manual and drawings. A long family history should not become an assumption that every older tuning instruction applies unchanged.

The supplied power system supports 2S and 3S without requiring a motor-and-controller conversion. For this buyer, that is useful future scope: begin with a deliberate lower-cell setup, then assess whether more power solves an actual desire. The manufacturer advertises 35-plus mph on 2S and 50-plus on 3S under its stated conditions. We have not measured either figure. Their role is to describe the available operating envelope, not to decide how fast the first backyard session ought to be.

Keep stock gearing while you learn the ordinary surface. Monster tires, grass drag and repeated acceleration all influence load, while a motor fan is useful only when its airflow remains clear. Inspect the fan and drivetrain after initial runs. If the truck continually bogs in thick growth, move to a more appropriate patch before changing gearing. A brushless-ready high-power package still deserves correct operation; it is not a reason to treat mechanical binding or resistance as something the battery should overpower.

04 /

Cell count, throttle limit and DSC are separate choices.

The SLT3 transmitter supplies steering, throttle and documented limiting options. DSC adds electronic stability-control modes through the installed system. Read the manual's descriptions carefully, because assistance mode and maximum motor output do different jobs. A lower throttle limit can make a first brushless session more manageable, while DSC changes the assistance behavior. Choosing both deliberately is more useful than leaving them unexamined and assuming the new truck will automatically make the correct corrections.

Start with a 2S route if it fits the intended use, a conservative limit and a broad practice circuit. Learn the trigger's neutral, braking and reverse sequence before adding speed. If you later change the assistance setting, keep the battery and route consistent so you can understand the change. A feature advertised for beginners should still be learned at the workbench. The available modes are setup tools, not an excuse to drive through the limited space of an unsuitable site.

Follow the correct LiPo operating and charging procedures, and secure the leads away from the rotating assemblies. This review assesses the control equipment for a buyer deliberately entering brushless monster driving. It does not measure correction effectiveness, radio range, response time or stopping distance. DSC also should not be confused with the AVC equipment found on some other ARRMA vehicles. The exact controller and receiver in this SKU determine which functions you are purchasing.

05 /

A compact tall truck needs a clear setup baseline.

The S662 metal-geared servo, independent suspension and oil-filled shocks are part of the current official package. Begin by inspecting that the steering moves freely, centers correctly and does not strain against a mechanical stop. A large monster tire can load a steering system when stationary or trapped beside a rock. Avoid holding steering into that obstruction. Before concluding that a servo replacement is necessary, identify whether the issue is a setting, mechanical damage or debris around the linkage.

The compact wheelbase and tall monster proportions create a reason to learn smooth inputs. They are not measured proof of unstable behavior, and we do not assign a rollover threshold. Use broad first turns on firm ground, then introduce tighter direction changes gradually. If the surface offers much more grip than the dirt route you started on, treat it as another setup context. Tire contact, speed and steering demand can change the vehicle's task without a single component being defective.

Make suspension changes against a repeated bump or turn and record the original positions. More spring preload does not automatically make a truck tougher, nor does a softer setup automatically improve every landing. Describe the behavior you are addressing before changing a part. The rating rewards documented adjustment and service access for the intended owner. It does not claim a shock-damping, steering-force or jump-control measurement from a drive test we have not conducted.

06 /

Monster proportions work best when rough ground remains driveable.

ARRMA presents the current dBoots tire and suspension package for all-terrain recreational use. I would begin on firm dirt, modest loose ground and short grass with an open braking area. Inspect the route for sharp debris, deep wheel-catching ruts and hard edges near a landing. Center clearance alone cannot describe every suspension part's path. A truck that looks tall on a product page can still catch a wheel or arm on an obstacle approached carelessly.

Tall or wet grass deserves separate attention. It can drag and wrap around rotating components, so compare short initial runs and inspect the buildup. The answer to persistent resistance is often a better patch of ground, rather than immediately fitting a larger pinion or choosing 3S. A crawler route is also a different use case. If the activity you want is inching through rocks with deliberate axle placement, compare a crawler instead of relying on the word monster to imply crawling control.

The independent RC Cars Guide coverage distinguishes current Granite versions using researched sources. It is useful generation context, not a hands-on result. We do not borrow earlier brushed Granite damage records or older 3S speed measurements. Our terrain conclusion follows from the current documented package and the route a buyer can inspect. The next useful evidence comes from repeatable sessions on that actual ground, which should decide whether a tire change or another vehicle format is worthwhile.

Carefully distinguish the four-wheel-drive version from the separate two-wheel-drive Vorteks. Similar names do not establish equivalent traction, battery trays or service layouts. BEFORE YOU ORDER /

The questions worth asking.

Which Granite is this?+

The red complete ARA4302V4T2 current 223S DSC4WD truck. Boost, MEGA, GROM and older 3S variants are different packages.

Is rechargeable power included?+

No. Choose a documented 2S or 3S hardcase LiPo with the proper connection and a compatible charger. The official package supplies transmitter AAs.

What is the battery-tray conflict?+

The current web table says 139mm length; the manual says 165mm. We preserve both and recommend actual fit confirmation before buying a pack near either limit.

Does it still use the old center arrangement?+

Current documentation identifies an all-metal geared center differential and its module. Do not apply an older slipper-based recipe without checking the exact assembly.

Does 3S need another motor or ESC?+

The fitted 223S system supports 2S and 3S. Correct pack fit, operating procedures and control setup are still required.

Is 50-plus mph our test result?+

No. It is a manufacturer claim for its specified 3S conditions. The editorial rating does not measure speed, crash resistance or reliability.
