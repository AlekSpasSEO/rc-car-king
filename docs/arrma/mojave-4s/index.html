THE OVERVIEW

The Mojave 4S is for a returning recreational driver who wants a substantial desert-truck RTR with a cockpit, inner body and four-wheel-drive mechanical package already coordinated. I would shortlist it when scale presentation is part of the enjoyment and there is open ground to use a longer truck. The reservation is ownership scale: at a listed 638mm length and 4.75kg weight, it asks for more space, carrying and service preparation than a small beginner vehicle, before the extra battery and charger are even considered.

The manufacturer's current feature list identifies EXB-derived CV shafts and metal input and ring gears. A differential lets connected wheel or drive paths accommodate different speeds, with the installed configuration determining how load is distributed. That matters when a desert truck crosses varied ground, but it does not establish a measured handling advantage by itself. Keep the factory arrangement while learning the regular route. If later changing fluids or components, state the actual behavior you want to alter and compare one change at a time. Another model's setting is not automatically correct for this chassis and tire combination.

The 3S route provides a documented alternative to 4S without requiring another motor and ESC. 05 /

Sway bars and adjustable links are useful tools for a repeatable route.

The current S652 metal-geared servo, adjustable turnbuckles, oil-filled aluminum shocks and front and rear sway bars are documented hardware. Check steering through its range and inspect free movement before introducing more load. Large tires pressed into an obstruction need room to turn, not a prolonged steering command against resistance. Verify body and suspension clearance with the full movement range, especially after any later tire or ride-height change.

A sway bar couples movement across an axle and changes the response when the wheels move differently. It can be useful for managing body movement, while also affecting how tires follow uneven terrain. Do not select a setting only because it seems firmer or more advanced. Establish a repeated corner and modest rough section, record the factory arrangement and compare one change across both. A setup that helps the smooth portion may give something away on the uneven part of the same route.

Adjustable links and shocks provide genuine setup scope, but they also make it easier to lose the baseline. Keep notes and inspect tire contact and wear. The manufacturer's scale body movement is a presentation claim, not a damping measurement supplied by this review. We reward the documented equipment and usable adjustment range for an interested owner. We do not report measured steering force, cornering pace, landing control or a suspension advantage over another desert truck.

06 /

A larger desert body needs a route proportionate to its footprint.

The dBoots Fortress LP tires, listed 37mm center clearance and long 380mm wheelbase describe the stock terrain package. Firm dirt and varied recreational ground are the first environments I would inspect. A desert silhouette does not mean loose sand of any depth or every rough site is equally appropriate. Walk the route for ruts, stones and hard edges that could catch an arm or wheel, and leave space for braking and recovery beyond the most exciting section.

Thick grass can still add drag to a brushless 4WD truck. Inspect buildup and cooling after shorter initial runs while learning the surface. More voltage is not the correct response to every obstruction. The detailed body and inner shell also create places where dirt can accumulate, so choose a route you can clean up after as well as drive. A larger vehicle makes that service task a more substantial part of the outing than it may have been on a small RTR.

Begin modestly, then let repeatable use identify which part of the route or setup actually needs changing.
