THE OVERVIEW

The Maxx 89086-4 is for a recreational basher who wants a serious four-wheel-drive monster truck in a more manageable footprint than Traxxas's largest vehicles. Its WideMaxx stance, substantial tires and 3S/4S-capable brushless system make the brief clear. I would shortlist it for a returning hobbyist or a deliberate step up from a smaller vehicle. The main reservation is the whole purchase: battery, charger, transport space and a suitable driving area all need to be settled before the car-only price means much.

02 /

The RTR truck still needs a deliberate LiPo-and-charger choice.

Traxxas includes the assembled vehicle with its finished clipless body, 540XL motor, VXL-4s controller, TQi radio, steering system and maintenance tools. The documented WideMaxx suspension and Sledgehammer tires are factory equipment on this current model. That makes it different from an older narrow truck with an optional widening kit. Verify the precise generation when comparing an old review, a used listing or a color variant advertised with an abbreviated title.

Supply a suitable 3S or 4S LiPo, compatible charger and four AA transmitter cells. The manufacturer states that NiMH batteries are not supported. A pack from a beginner NiMH truck therefore does not complete this purchase, even if you are accustomed to the brand. Check the 186 × 48 × 43mm battery compartment and the retention arrangement against the exact pack. Capacity, connector and cell count are each relevant; none by itself establishes a complete fit.

Establish the battery and charging plan before the truck arrives. Then inspect wheel fasteners, body retention, lead routing and free steering movement on the bench. The factory assembly reduces work, but does not eliminate setup and inspection. Begin with conservative controls on an open route rather than installing speed gearing for the inaugural run. A useful first session confirms steering, braking and normal drivetrain behavior, leaving bigger jumps and power changes for a stage when you understand the stock vehicle.

03 /

The current Maxx is built around 3S/4S power and a substantial driveline.

The 540XL is a sensorless four-pole brushless motor rated at 2400Kv by the manufacturer. The VXL-4s controller supports the documented 3S/4S LiPo route. Shaft-driven four-wheel drive, sealed differentials, a Cush Drive arrangement and Torque-Biasing Center Drive belong to the published package. These are specific mechanisms that manage how power reaches the tires. Do not replace their names with a generic assumption that every truck has the same center differential or slipper arrangement.

The power system's capability does not dictate the battery you must start with. Traxxas documents reduced performance on 3S and recommends 4S for maximum performance. For a step-up owner, that provides a sensible way to select an initial configuration while learning the vehicle's size and responses. It is a buying option, not a promise that every lower-cell session is easy. The actual ground, available room and control choices still determine how approachable the first runs are.

The official 60-plus mph figure requires a 4S pack and optional gearing; the feature copy separately describes a lower stock-configuration claim. We have not measured either. Keep the gearing conditions attached to the numbers instead of turning them into a universal out-of-box speed. A rough-ground basher can be worthwhile without operating as a speed-run car. Stock gearing and an appropriate surface are a better first combination than a tall pinion chosen to match the largest headline on the page.

04 /

Assistance and output reduction address different parts of the session.

The TQi transmitter and TSM receiver provide adjustable electronic steering assistance, while the controller's documented profiles offer a separate way to structure power use. Read the current manual for the setting procedure rather than assuming the factory configuration fits every driver. TSM should be understood as an aid within the available tire grip and mechanical setup. It cannot make a narrow site wider, eliminate all slides or substitute for a clean stopping area.

Use a simple route for the first control comparison. Practice accelerating gradually, lifting before a broad turn and stopping without rushing to reverse. Keep the battery and assistance setting consistent while learning that baseline. If you change the cell count and steering assistance together, it becomes harder to identify what caused a difference. The Maxx's substantial size makes deliberate setup worthwhile even for someone with past RC experience on smaller vehicles.

The receiver and ESC have documented telemetry capabilities, but the optional wireless Bluetooth module should not be assumed included. App-compatible and app-connected out of the box are different equipment descriptions. Count the actual accessory if those features are central to your purchase. Our controls score assesses the available documented settings for a returning recreational monster-truck owner. We do not report measured radio range, assistance effectiveness, brake distance or telemetry accuracy for this vehicle.

05 /

WideMaxx describes a factory stance, not a cornering result.

The current model includes WideMaxx suspension arms, GT-Maxx aluminum-bodied shocks and a substantial 2090 steering servo. The manufacturer's feature copy identifies the widened suspension arrangement as 40mm broader than its narrower configuration. Those are meaningful purchase facts. They do not establish a measured rollover threshold or guarantee that abrupt steering on high-grip pavement will end well. Match your first turns to the vehicle's size and learn its response progressively.

The large tires can load the steering when stationary or pressed into an obstacle. Check mechanical travel and keep debris from the bellcranks. If the truck steers inconsistently, inspect the assembly before replacing the servo or adding radio trim. The manufacturer describes preset gear-mesh positions and a modular motor cradle elsewhere in the chassis; similarly, the steering should be serviced using its actual drawings rather than a generic monster-truck recipe. Correct assembly is a more useful starting point than a speculative upgrade list.

Threaded shocks let you adjust ride height consistently, but suspension changes should serve the terrain. A setup for broad paved turns is not necessarily the setup you want for uneven dirt and modest jumps. Record the original settings, pick a repeated feature and change one item. A heavier battery or different tire can change more than appearance. Our rating rewards the documented factory suspension package and service information, without claiming we measured its damping, steering force or landing control.

06 /

Give the Sledgehammers a route larger than the truck's shadow.

The current Sledgehammer tire package, broad footprint and listed 67mm center clearance suit the monster-truck buying brief. Dirt, rough recreational ground and short grass are reasonable first environments to assess. Look for deep ruts, hard edges and obstacles that can catch a suspension arm even when the center of the chassis clears. Four-wheel drive helps supply propulsion at both ends; it does not make every obstacle harmless or prevent tires from throwing debris into the vehicle.

Grass can still add drag and collect around moving parts. Start with stock gearing and short inspection intervals while learning the site. If the truck consistently struggles through thick growth, a better patch of ground may be the answer before more gearing or another battery. For jumps, choose modest launches with visible landings and space beyond them. We do not have a crash test that establishes a safe height, so the equipment list should not become a stunt prescription.

We use it as an additional view of the matching broad generation rather than copying a speed or damage outcome. 07 /

A large body hides enough chassis that regular removal matters.

The current shell uses a clipless pivoting-latch arrangement with reinforcements. The truck also has bumpers, skid pieces, side protection and a wheelie-bar carry-handle feature. Those are practical access and protection components, not a measured crash-survival guarantee. Learn the release motion and inspect that the body seats correctly after a battery change. A finished shell is convenient, but its condition still deserves attention when the vehicle tumbles or rubs against a rough landing area.

The modular chassis and published exploded views help you understand where the power system and driveline are housed. After sessions, inspect wheel nuts, tire bonding, shafts and shock seals, then clean around the motor and controller fans. A large amount of cooling hardware is less useful when debris blocks it. Check the battery restraint and leads carefully before fitting the next pack. The larger vehicle also benefits from a bench or stand that supports it securely while you work.

Follow the owner's manual after wet use. Waterproof electronics do not mean a dirty mechanical assembly can be stored without drying and inspection. We have not established long-term reliability or measured replacement-part availability. Check the supply of the exact generation's service components with the shop you would use. The same Maxx name on an old article, upgraded chassis or different Ultimate package does not automatically establish compatibility for everything fitted to this reviewed truck.

A convincing step-up truck when the complete ownership plan is ready.

I would consider the red Maxx 89086-4 for a returning hobbyist or step-up basher with open outdoor space, a deliberate LiPo charging plan and interest in a substantial 4WD monster. The factory WideMaxx hardware, current tires, documented service layout and adjustable assistance provide a credible package. The reservation is the practical scale of ownership: this is a heavy, broad vehicle despite the nominal 1/10 label, and its car-only price omits essential power equipment.

BEFORE YOU ORDER /

The questions worth asking.

What exact Maxx generation is covered?+

The red complete 89086-4 with factory WideMaxx suspension. Mini Maxx, an older narrow Maxx and Maxx Ultimate are different purchases.

Can I use a NiMH battery?+

Traxxas states NiMH is unsupported. Choose a documented 3S or 4S LiPo and appropriate charger, with correct tray fit and connector.

Is 60+mph the stock claim?+

The manufacturer's 60+mph figure requires 4S and optional gearing. Keep that condition attached to the number; we have not speed-tested it.

Does the app module come in the box?+

The stock radio and ESC have documented capabilities, but the wireless Bluetooth module is an optional accessory. App-compatible does not mean every connection accessory is supplied.

How large is it?+

Current official dimensions are 572mm long, 405mm wide and 232mm tall, with approximately 4.7kg listed weight. Check physical storage and carrying space rather than relying on nominal scale.

Is it tougher than a Kraton?+

We have no controlled head-to-head damage test. Compare documented hardware, size, power basket and support, without treating either feature list as a measured crash ranking.
