V3300 and VX3 identify the current configuration

The current manufacturer identifies a 3300Kv V3300 sensorless brushless motor paired with VX3. That is the equipment this review describes, not a BL-2s setup inferred from the related Drift car. Kv is a motor speed-per-volt label, not a measured vehicle speed or a guarantee of available grip. The actual car adds its gearing, tires, supported battery configuration and driving surface. Those details must remain connected when interpreting any performance claim.

The steel center driveshaft sends power to both axles, with sealed steel-gear differentials. The stock on-road tires and low chassis position make it a street-car proposition. AWD does not eliminate understeer, tire limits or the need to brake before an obstacle, and we have no physical handling data to turn those mechanisms into a universal grip ranking. It is also not evidence that the vehicle will behave like the dedicated rear-drive drift version on its own specialist tires.

Traxxas advertises different speed potential on 2S and 3S without requiring a gearing change. Those are manufacturer claims, not RC Car King measurements, and they do not determine a suitable first venue. Use the factory gearing and an appropriate supported setup while learning. 04 /

TSM helps with steering; it does not choose the route

The TQi Sport radio provides the control system, and Traxxas Stability Management supplies documented steering assistance. TSM responds to the vehicle's motion to help with directional control; it does not see pedestrians, identify a curb or create additional stopping distance. Learn how to enable and disable it from the exact radio instructions and understand its effect in a clear area. We are not describing a measured correction angle or claiming that it prevents crashes.

VX3's Sport, Race and Training profiles provide different operating responses. Training limits forward and reverse output while retaining braking according to the manual. Race omits reverse. Those distinctions are useful for planning a beginner setup and interpreting behavior, but they are not a universal button sequence for every Traxxas controller. Read the actual selection and control-check instructions before changing the profile, and check direction and neutral behavior using the prescribed precautions.

Start with gentle inputs, generous separation and an uncomplicated route. Stop if steering strains, throttle response is abnormal or a protection indication appears. Make one documented change at a time rather than resetting several controls until the car moves. A useful first session establishes predictable steering, braking and recovery at the selected configuration. It does not require exploring full supported power, recording a speed run or proving that the body styling looks convincing in a powerslide photograph.

05 /

Adjustability is a reason to learn a baseline

The platform uses oil-filled Ultra Shocks, captured suspension links, a low-CG chassis and a 2057 metal-gear steering servo with an integrated saver. The modular chassis arrangement and adjustable battery retention are documented features. They help explain the equipment and access layout, not a measured claim about damping quality or steering precision. We have not compared shock settings, cornering speeds or tire compounds on a physical test car.

Choose a repeatable paved route before changing suspension settings. If the car behaves unexpectedly, check the surface, tires, free movement and the actual controls before ordering tuning parts. A stone caught in the chassis or a tire fouling a body is not a suspension setup problem. Keep steering travel within the applicable instructions and do not hold it against binding. The presence of a servo saver is not permission to use the steering system as a lever against a curb.

An organized race class introduces additional questions: dimensions, tires, power rules and permissible assistance. The Ready-To-Race trademark and our on-road category label do not answer those rules. Ask the local organizer before buying the car for a specific class. For recreational use, the stronger comparison is whether the documented chassis, controls and course fit meet your intended activity. Avoid turning a list of adjustable hardware into a promise that the stock package suits every track.

06 /

Pavement quality is part of the specification

With a manufacturer-listed 12mm center ground clearance, the 240SX needs a much cleaner surface than a trail truck. Broken asphalt, gravel, tall grass and abrupt driveway transitions are poor starting assumptions. Walk the proposed driving area first, identify a clear run-out and keep traffic and pedestrians separate. Small debris can reach the chassis even when it appears insignificant beside a parked full-size car. AWD changes the driven wheels, not the height of the underside.

Begin with a broad route and the documented learning controls. Do not use a public-road speed figure as a venue recommendation or assume a large battery makes braking space less important. A supported lower-power configuration still requires enough room to stop and recover the vehicle. If the available paved area is cramped, a smaller complete on-road car or a different activity may be a better purchase than a large street car that is rarely driven comfortably.

Wet-operation claims have conditions too. The exact R00 manual distinguishes water-resistant equipment from a submersible or completely waterproof vehicle and sets separate transmitter, battery and maintenance limits. Start dry, and consult those exact instructions before any other environment. We do not transplant a K10 battery rule, give an immersion recommendation or invent an aftermarket sealing solution. A simple dry venue makes early control checks and post-run inspection easier to interpret without adding weather and contamination variables.

BEFORE YOU ORDER /

The questions worth asking.

Is this the same car as the 4-Tec Drift Nissan 240SX?+

No. This 93424-4 is the shaft-AWD street car with V3300 motor and VX3 controller. The 105247-4 Drift package has a different rear-drive brief despite similar body styling.

Does the 93424-4 include a battery and charger?+

No. Add a supported 2S or 3S LiPo, compatible charger and four AA alkaline transmitter batteries. Check physical fit, retention and the actual equipment instructions.

Can the VX3 use NiMH batteries?+

The exact current product documentation specifies 2S or 3S LiPo and says NiMH is not supported. Advice for an older brushed 4-Tec does not establish compatibility here.

Does TSM prevent crashes?+

No. It is steering assistance, not obstacle detection or an automatic braking system. A suitable clear venue and driver control remain necessary.

What is the car’s verified weight and motor shaft size?+

They are not independently verified here. Traxxas lists conflicting 2.28lb/1.30kg weight values and 3mm/5mm shaft descriptions. Confirm the actual vehicle and motor where those measurements affect a decision.

Has RC Car King measured its speed or compared it on a track?+

No. Advertised speed potential belongs to the manufacturer. This is a research-led buying assessment, with no measured speed, braking, lap-time, runtime or competition-class claim.
