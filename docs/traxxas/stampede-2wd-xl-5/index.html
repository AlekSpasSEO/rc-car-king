02 /

The included NiMH system removes two major beginner decisions.

The official box contents include the assembled Stampede, TQ transmitter, fitted Titan motor and XL-5 controller, 3000mAh seven-cell 8.4V NiMH pack, four-amp USB-C charger and maintenance tools. The body and tires are finished. You can concentrate on learning the transmitter and checking the truck instead of choosing a power system. That is a meaningful benefit for a buyer starting without existing RC equipment, and it should be counted when comparing the price with an RTR whose drive battery is extra.

Four AA alkaline cells and an adequate USB-C power source remain necessary. Traxxas recommends a 45-watt adapter and its power cable. A USB-shaped socket does not establish the power your charger can draw, so use the charging instructions to confirm the supply. Put the adapter and cable on the gift checklist if they are not already available. The term ready-to-run describes the vehicle assembly and included equipment; it still leaves a short but important preparation stage.

Before driving, check the battery restraint, lead clearance, body retention and wheel security. The tall tires make it especially easy to see whether each corner turns freely when the truck is raised. Follow the radio startup procedure and begin with an open, level route. Do not make the first run a test of the largest obstacle the tires can reach. Establish normal steering, braking and motor behavior on simple ground so later changes have a useful reference.

03 /

Tall tires change the load, so keep the gearing decision conservative.

The Titan 12T 550 drives the Magnum 272 transmission and rear wheels through a slipper clutch and planetary differential. The manufacturer's current specifications list 48-pitch gearing. The power path is relatively easy to identify in the service drawings, which makes this an approachable learning platform. Rear-wheel drive keeps the front assembly simpler than a 4WD truck, but also means the rear tires must supply all propulsion when the truck meets loose or uneven ground.

Monster tires are useful for crossing small irregularities, yet they do not make gearing irrelevant. A taller or heavier replacement tire can change the effective load on the motor even when the pinion stays untouched. Begin with the documented factory combination and inspect the motor and drivetrain after short runs. Do not force the truck repeatedly against an obstacle that stops the wheels. Choosing a better line or a different surface can be a more appropriate response than feeding in more throttle.

Traxxas advertises 30-plus mph with the seven-cell pack. This review does not verify that speed or turn it into a crash-resistance claim. The useful motor question is whether the stock package suits your ordinary terrain. A brushless-ready transmission describes potential compatibility, not an included brushless system. If conversion is already certain, count the controller, motor, battery equipment and possible associated changes against the cost of a factory brushless monster truck before treating this version as the economical route.

04 /

Use reduced power to understand the tall truck's responses.

The TQ two-channel 2.4GHz radio controls steering and throttle. The XL-5 provides Sport, Race and Training profiles, with Training reducing forward and reverse output while keeping braking. That is a useful tool for a tall first monster truck: it lets you practice direction changes and stopping without making every trigger movement a full-power event. This package has no TSM electronic assistance. Your choice of line, throttle and steering remains responsible for what the truck does.

Set the driving profile before the session. Start with wide turns and enough room to finish braking on the chosen surface. A wheelie bar is included, but do not turn its presence into a promise that every acceleration will behave as you expect. The bar supports the documented monster-truck package; it is not a substitute for learning the throttle. Practice smooth launches, then separate that exercise from tighter cornering so you can identify which input caused an unwanted response.

The supplied NiMH pack also provides a straightforward controller baseline. If you later change to a compatible 2S LiPo, use the manual to establish low-voltage detection and the correct charging method. Physical fit and connector compatibility are only part of that change. Our controls score recognizes the simple radio and documented profiles for a first recreational monster-truck buyer. We have no measured radio range, throttle response or stopping-distance data for this exact blue vehicle.

05 /

Clearance is useful; rapid direction changes still deserve care.

Traxxas lists the 2056 steering servo, spring-loaded servo saver and oil-filled Ultra Shocks. The current package adds heavy-duty suspension components. These parts make a credible beginner specification, but our review does not infer an indestructible steering assembly. Check full movement with the truck supported and inspect for binding, trapped vegetation and tire contact. Center the steering before using trim to address a small offset, and repair damaged links rather than trying to electronically hide the problem.

The listed 102mm center ground clearance is one of the Stampede's defining facts. It is clearance at the center of the vehicle, not under every suspension arm or wheel component. The tall chassis and tires also make weight transfer worth understanding. Keep your first turns broad, especially on grippy pavement. Abrupt steering while accelerating is a different exercise from driving over a small dirt bump, and there is no reason to learn both at maximum pace in the same first minute.

Use a repeatable patch of ground when adjusting ride height or shock setup. Document the starting position and avoid changing several items together. If the truck rolls in a particular turn, compare speed, radius and grip before buying springs or lowering it. Suspension settings should answer the job you actually use the truck for. A configuration that suits a smooth parking area may sacrifice the clearance that attracted you in the first place.

06 /

The right route gives the monster shape a real purpose.

The current official page identifies high-profile Terra Groove monster tires and a tall monocoque chassis. That combination belongs on a shortlist for dirt, modest rough ground and short grass. Walk the intended route first. Look for stones that could wedge near the wheels, deep ruts and growth likely to wrap around shafts. The truck's stance can help with some surface irregularities, but choosing a clear line is still part of driving it rather than a sign the equipment has failed.

Dense, wet lawn creates drag regardless of the appealing ground-clearance figure. Begin with shorter sessions and inspect how much material accumulates. If the truck constantly bogs, choose another area instead of holding full throttle through the resistance. A crawler is a separate alternative when the goal is very slow rock work; the Stampede's recreational powertrain and tires should not be presented as equivalent simply because both vehicles are used outdoors.

Historical independent XL-5 Stampede coverage from RC-News describes earlier versions and supplies context for the model family. It does not prove the current 36254-8 has the same tested outcomes, body mounting or charging package. We deliberately do not transfer those results. The buying assessment here rests on the current manufacturer's terrain hardware and the owner's intended ground. That distinction matters when a familiar vehicle name spans many years of suspension, electronics and package revisions.

07 /

Treat the body, wheelie bar and bumpers as serviceable equipment.

The clipless finished shell gives the current truck a convenient battery-access arrangement. LED-ready bumpers and the included wheelie bar are documented hardware; optional lighting is not installed merely because a bumper can accept it. Learn the body-release motion and inspect the latch seating after putting it back. A finished body removes the painting project from the first purchase, while the hidden mounting keeps the truck's appearance clean. Neither feature establishes a measured body life.

After running, remove the shell and check the upper chassis, motor guard, rear driveshafts and suspension. Tall tires can fling dirt into places that were clean on the bench. Inspect tire bonding and wheel nuts, then look for shock leakage or bent shafts. If the wheelie bar or bumper has taken a hit, identify damage before it becomes a loose part during the next session. The manufacturer provides parts lists and exploded views so those assemblies can be understood by name and number.

Waterproof electronics need the manual's cleaning and drying routine. Do not leave a muddy truck packed away after wet use, and keep the charger in its intended environment. We do not have a long-term reliability log or a survey of parts availability. Check nearby support before buying for someone who will need help with repairs. A modest service routine and a shop willing to explain a drawing are more useful beginner advantages than a speculative list of upgrades supposedly needed by every Stampede.

08 /

Value improves when the included charging equipment fits your household.

The vehicle reference is dated and identifies the complete 36254-8 package. The supplied NiMH pack and USB-C charger belong in the included column; transmitter AA cells and an adequate adapter and cable belong in the completion column. Extras do not receive invented prices here. An honest startup basket is useful even when it cannot give one universal subtotal, because a household with the right USB supply has a different remaining purchase from a household starting with nothing.

Keep a second drive battery optional until charging turnaround becomes a real issue. Verify the tray dimensions and restraint arrangement before ordering another pack. Capacity alone cannot tell you whether it fits, and a matching plug does not establish chemistry compatibility. Likewise, budget tools based on the service you will perform. A better driver set can make frequent work more pleasant, while the included tools give you a starting point for the prescribed checks.

The value question changes if you intend to replace the brushed system immediately. Write down the complete conversion basket and compare a factory brushless monster truck. Do not count the same supplied battery as a benefit while simultaneously planning never to use it. The Stampede makes most sense as a coordinated first outdoor package. A buyer already equipped with suitable RC batteries and charging hardware may find a battery-free RTR closer to their actual needs.

09 /

Monster height, stadium proportions and four driven wheels are different choices.

The ARRMA Gorgon MEGA battery-inclusive RTR is the closest broad alternative for a first rear-drive monster-truck buyer. It uses a different chassis, tire package and electronics arrangement, and its precise red package has remaining-stock considerations discussed in that review. Compare current verified package contents and charging supplies rather than old launch prices. Both can reduce the first battery-selection burden. We have no direct handling or durability comparison between them, so the sensible deciding factors are local support, terrain fit and the exact equipment you need.

The Rustler XL-5 37254-8 is the alternative when you want a lower stadium truck with exposed wheels. Its shorter overall height changes the shape of the driving project, while its included battery-and-charger concept remains similar. Choose the Stampede because you need the tall monster format, not because the Rustler's different proportions are defective. For firmer circuits and a compact shelf footprint, the stadium route deserves a fair look.

The ARRMA Granite 223S adds four-wheel drive, brushless 2S/3S capability and DSC assistance, with a battery and charger purchased separately. That is a substantial change in both the traction and ownership package. If front-wheel propulsion or factory brushless power is already on your requirements list, compare the Granite's full basket now. Avoid buying a rear-drive brushed truck solely to spend the following month trying to make it a different category of vehicle.

10 /

A first monster truck that earns its place through the complete package.

I would recommend considering the Stampede 36254-8 for a newcomer with usable outdoor ground who actively wants the tall rear-drive monster format. The supplied battery and charger, reduced-power mode, finished body and documented service resources give it a coherent start. The tall clearance figure should be interpreted correctly, and the first sessions should teach smooth direction changes before ambitious stunts. This is a purchase recommendation for that buyer, not a claim that every yard or every novice will produce the same experience.

Skip it for slow technical crawling, a predetermined race class or a guaranteed immediate brushless conversion. Shortlist it when stock monster-truck driving is the desired activity and the charging package is genuinely useful. Begin with the factory tire and gear combination, inspect after runs and price upgrades only when actual use identifies the need. The Stampede's charm is approachable outdoor play, but its strongest buying argument is that the vehicle and most of the first power equipment arrive as one understandable package.

BEFORE YOU ORDER /

The questions worth asking.

Is this a Stampede 4X4?+

No. SKU 36254-8 is the brushed 2WD package with rear-wheel drive. The name also appears on different four-wheel-drive vehicles.

What does the 102mm clearance describe?+

The current manufacturer lists center ground clearance. It does not mean every suspension and wheel component clears obstacles by that amount.

Does the box include charging equipment?+

A NiMH battery and four-amp USB-C charger are supplied. You still need four AA transmitter cells and suitable USB-C input power.

Are lights factory fitted?+

The current package documents LED-ready bumpers. Readiness for optional lighting does not establish that an accessory light kit is installed.

Can I use it instead of a crawler?+

Choose this for recreational monster-truck driving. A slow technical rock route calls for comparison with a vehicle built around crawling gearing and controls.

Are old Stampede reviews directly applicable?+

They provide historical context. Current 36254-8 documentation establishes this generation; earlier test speed, damage and package results are not carried across.
