01 /

A lifted pickup with more than one way to drive it

The Traxxas TRX-4 Chevrolet K10 High Trail Edition is a deliberate style and terrain choice. Its tall stance, large-looking tires and long pickup body are the appeal. Underneath, model 92056-4 supplies portal axles, a two-speed transmission and remotely locking front and rear differentials. That makes it a configurable scale trail truck, but it does not prove that adding height automatically improves every kind of crawling. The route you want to drive still has the deciding vote.

This review concerns the full-size 1/10 TRX-4 in black, not the much smaller TRX-4M K10 and not a different standard-height Chevy release. The wheelbase is 336mm and the listed weight is 3.35kg. Those figures affect course choice, carrying and storage before you touch the transmitter. If your reference point is a small indoor crawler, verify the physical footprint instead of buying by body shape and expecting a similar ownership experience.

Our assessment is based on the exact Traxxas product page and 92056-4 R01 manual, with limited attributable independent context. We have not driven the K10, measured its rollover angle or tested its electronics. The editorial case is strongest for someone who wants this lifted scale pickup and will use its drivetrain controls thoughtfully on varied trails. A buyer concentrating on narrow technical lines and low body position should compare other complete trucks before choosing the extra height.

02 /

RTR means assembled; the power basket remains yours

Traxxas supplies the completed truck, Titan 550 21-turn brushed motor, XL-5 HV speed controller, TQi radio system and installed steering and drivetrain-control servos. The licensed body is finished and the High Trail lift is already fitted. You are not buying a bare chassis or a replacement shell. You are also not buying a battery-and-charger-complete bundle just because the vehicle is described as ready to run.

The drive battery, an appropriate charger and four AA alkaline transmitter cells are separate requirements. Traxxas specifies support for six- or seven-cell NiMH packs or two- to three-cell LiPo packs under the actual model and ESC instructions. Voltage compatibility alone does not establish a complete match: check physical fit, retention, connector, charging requirements and the correct low-voltage-detection setting. The listed battery compartment is 159 × 47 × 23mm, so a pack that sounds electrically suitable may still be the wrong physical purchase.

Optional equipment needs the same clarity. Working Pro Scale LED lighting and the wireless Traxxas Link module are separate additions, not benefits silently included in the base vehicle reference. The official black photographs identify the complete vehicle and its finish; they are not our unboxing or evidence that every seller includes an accessory bundle. Write down the exact supplied and required equipment before comparing this K10 with an RTR whose box includes drive power and charging.

03 /

Low gear and diff locks are choices, not decoration

The Titan 550 21T reverse-rotation brushed motor and XL-5 HV ESC form the documented stock electrical system. The transmission provides remotely selected low and high gears, while the axle differentials can be opened or locked from the radio. Portal reductions place the axle centerline above the wheel centers. Those are meaningful design distinctions, but they do not let us invent wheel torque, motor temperature, runtime or a battery-specific maximum speed.

Use the manual's operating guidance. Low gear is the appropriate starting point for crawling and climbing. Traxxas advises reducing speed before changing from high into low to reduce transmission strain. Its manual also explains why low gear belongs in low-speed work rather than assuming the motor's ability to pull in high gear makes that the best long-term choice. Learn the switch operation and the actual selected gear on a clear course before approaching a difficult obstacle.

Differential locks provide another conditional tool. The manual describes open differentials as useful for steering and ordinary driving, with locking available when traction calls for it. If the truck is stuck with a single wheel spinning, release the throttle and let the wheels stop before locking; then apply throttle gently while the mechanism engages. Avoid treating every lock switch position as a permanent improvement. Steering and drivetrain load change with the selection, so the right setting depends on the line you are attempting.

04 /

Learn the stock profiles before adding connectivity

The supplied TQi four-channel transmitter and 6533 receiver support the functions that distinguish this package. The red rocker controls the transmission and the top switch operates the differential locks as described in the manual. Cruise control is also documented through the multi-function control. It can maintain a selected driving command, but it is not obstacle detection or an autonomous climbing aid. Keep direct control and an escape route while learning the truck.

The XL-5 HV offers Sport, Race, Training, Trail and Crawl profiles. Those names describe different throttle, braking and reverse behavior; Crawl's hill-hold and immediate reverse response should not be assumed identical to another mode. Training limits forward and reverse power while retaining braking according to the manual. Use the exact selection instructions if changing profiles, and confirm behavior on clear level ground. This review does not replace the controller's programming sequence or recommend an undocumented custom setting.

Check the steering, trim, battery selection and correct low-voltage protection before a drive. The stock metal-gear 2075X steering servo is documented, but that alone does not establish a measured steering-force result on every surface. If something binds or fails to respond, stop and diagnose the setup rather than leaning on the throttle. Wireless app connectivity requires the separate Link module. The truck's main appeal should make sense with the supplied radio before optional electronics become part of the purchase justification.

05 /

Clearance changes the obstacle problem, not the laws of balance

The factory Long Arm Lift Kit, extended steel links, revised servo mounting and 110mm GTS aluminum shocks create the High Trail stance. Traxxas markets the lift as adding more than an inch of clearance versus a standard TRX-4 arrangement. The product table lists 102mm center ground clearance. These are manufacturer figures and claims, not our measurements. Neither figure guarantees that a particular rock, log or ledge can be crossed without contact.

The 336mm wheelbase also matters. It creates a different breakover and turning problem from a shorter truck, while the taller body position changes the balance considerations on side slopes. Our practical inference is to choose lines deliberately and begin on low-consequence obstacles. We have not measured a maximum sidehill angle or conducted a rollover comparison. It would be misleading to translate the attractive stance into a numerical stability verdict from photographs alone.

Remote differential control can help you decide how much traction to request while preserving steering where possible, but it does not cancel geometry. Begin with a simple route, observe the truck's actual behavior and adjust the driving line before changing hardware. The stock 2.2-inch wheels are part of this package; that figure is wheel diameter, not a quoted overall tire diameter. No unverified tire measurement or universal suspension recipe is needed to explain why this is a lifted trail pickup rather than a low competition crawler.

06 /

Dry trails first; read the wet-use limits literally

Dry dirt paths, rocky trail sections and deliberately chosen crawling obstacles are a sensible starting brief. Leave room around the full-size truck and avoid routes where a mistake sends it toward people, animals, traffic or an unrecoverable drop. Low gear and careful inputs are more useful starting tools than using high gear to charge at an obstacle. The truck's mass and size make recovery and carrying part of the route plan, not an afterthought.

Water resistance needs precise language. The exact R01 manual says the vehicle must not be treated as submersible or completely waterproof, and the transmitter is not water resistant. Its wet-running precautions also explicitly prohibit LiPo use in wet conditions and require additional care and maintenance. Follow that limit even though the stock ESC supports LiPo for appropriate operation. Electrical voltage compatibility is not permission to ignore the manual's separate environmental restrictions.

Do not turn mud or water photographs into a universal capability claim. The manual also excludes saltwater and contaminated-water exposure and explains the maintenance burden after wet running. We recommend a dry first venue because it lets you learn the drivetrain without adding those variables. If you later choose an environment allowed by the actual instructions, follow all battery, vehicle and component limits together. We have not performed immersion tests, established sealing reliability or verified an aftermarket wet-use workaround.

BEFORE YOU ORDER /

The questions worth asking.

Is this the small TRX-4M K10?+

No. Model 92056-4 is the full-size 1/10 TRX-4 High Trail with 336mm wheelbase, portal axles, remote two-speed transmission and remote differential locks.

Are a battery and charger included?+

No. The stock vehicle includes the motor, ESC and TQi radio, but needs supported drive power, a compatible charger and four AA alkaline transmitter cells.

Can I leave it in high gear for crawling?+

The exact manual directs low gear for climbing and crawling and advises slowing before shifting from high to low. Learn and follow its transmission instructions.

Should I lock both differentials all the time?+

No universal setting is best. The manual describes open diffs for ordinary steering and locks when traction requires them. Release the throttle and stop a spinning wheel before locking when stuck, then apply throttle gently.

Can I run a LiPo through wet terrain?+

The exact R01 manual’s wet-use section says not to use LiPo batteries in wet conditions. It also says the vehicle is not submersible or completely waterproof, the transmitter is not water resistant, and extra maintenance is required.

What is the truck’s overall length?+

The current Traxxas text table lists 22.20 inches and 526mm, which do not agree. We have not independently measured it. Confirm actual overall length before buying close-fitting storage.

Is your rating based on a trail test?+

No. It is a research-led editorial buyer-fit assessment with five equally weighted, reasoned categories. No physical climbing, rollover, speed, crash or runtime result is claimed.
