Traxxas Slash 2WD Brushless VX3 review

The black 58434-4 pairs a low chassis and composite GTR shocks with new 2S/3S electronics. It makes the most sense on a wide course with room for a rear-drive truck.

01 /

Identify the 58434-4 before comparing any Slash prices

The black 58434-4 is the 2026 Slash 2WD Brushless with the VX3 controller and Velineon V3200. The name Slash now appears on everything from a brushed short-course truck to factory dirt oval cars. This one has the short-course shell, a low chassis and rear drive. If the listing says XL-5, BL-2s, VXL-3s or 4X4, stop and look at the model number instead of trying to reconcile two different power or drivetrain tables.

Greg Vogel’s RC Driver coverage of the 2026 truck shows why the motor is only half the buying decision. The update combines the VX3 and V3200 with a different chassis position, composite GTR dampers and heavy-duty suspension equipment. It makes sense to buy that combination if you want to adjust the truck rather than turn the older 2WD Slash into a parts project. Anyone mainly looking for easy neighborhood driving should count how often the low chassis or extra setup will actually matter.

02 /

The radio and tools are in the box; the drive power is not

The assembled truck arrives with the painted black body, motor and speed control installed, steering servo, receiver, TQi Sport transmitter and the listed maintenance tools. The drive battery, compatible balance charger and four AAs are separate. Those missing pieces are what turn the price into a usable car. If you own a brushed Slash with a supplied NiMH pack, the vehicles can share a badge without sharing the power you planned to bring.

The VX3 manual specifies one 2S or 3S Traxxas iD LiPo with the Traxxas High-Current connector. Check the pack table in the manual rather than matching only the connector or the voltage. The retailer lists a 166 by 49 by 28 mm battery compartment, but the listed pack choices and installed hold-down are the better decision points. The cable must clear the latch and sit without being crushed under the shell. A pack that wedges in or presses against the wiring is a different problem from a pack the chart approves.

03 /

Follow power through the 272 and its adjustable slipper

Traxxas puts a V3200 sensorless brushless motor behind the chassis and sends its power through the Magnum 272 transmission. The adjustable slipper is built around the spur and can absorb some shock and limit how abruptly torque arrives at the rear wheels. It cannot put any of that torque through the front tires. The gear differential lets the two rear tires turn at different speeds in a bend. A truck that swings its tail under an abrupt input can be doing what a rear-drive truck does rather than signaling that the differential is broken.

The 32-pitch gearing and 5 mm motor shaft are reasons to shop by the 58434-4 drawing rather than buying a pinion under the word Slash. The parts sheet includes 54T and 56T spurs and several compatible pinions. Its list is a parts catalog, not a prescription to fit the largest one. The exact manual uses a 2.72 internal multiplier for final drive and warns that its chart includes combinations that are not suitable for every application. Keep the supplied combination for initial running and verify the installed teeth before recording any final ratio.

If you have the truck open already, make a note of the 5 mm shaft and the factory gear adapter. Those two details matter if a previous owner has removed the adapter or changed the pinion. The gear cover should go back without rubbing, and the teeth need the mesh described by the manual. Avoid turning up the slipper to hide a transmission noise. A sound that arrives only under load and a bind you can feel with the truck switched off lead to different checks, and each deserves its own inspection.

04 /

Start with one 2S pack and learn what each control changes

The first setup I would choose is a supported 2S pack, the stock gears and a familiar surface with generous braking distance. The manual lists the 2827X 3000 mAh and several larger 2S iD packs, plus two specific 3S choices. It also lists the 2970 AC balance charger. A 3S pack is an option after you know the route; the VX3 does not require it to operate. Keep the manufacturer’s battery protections in place and use the charging instructions that go with the pack and charger.

The Traxxas Stability Management setting adjusts steering correction. Training mode is a separate drive profile that limits forward and reverse output. Race removes reverse; it does not certify eligibility for a race. Learn the radio’s trim and range check from the exact manual before changing either setting. If the truck heads sideways when you get back on the trigger, make the next pass with a gentler exit before adjusting the radio. Steering assistance can help correct heading; it does not create rear grip on a dusty patch.

05 /

Use the GTR collars to establish a repeatable stance

Threaded collars let the buyer adjust spring preload on the composite GTR shocks without stacking clip spacers. The effect you see at rest is a change in where the chassis sits in its travel. A collar does not change the spring’s rate or replace the damping oil. Start with a consistent left and right position on each axle and judge clearance and sag with the intended pack installed. If the truck lands frequently on the chassis with one corner sticking, check that corner for damage or binding before dialing up preload all around.

The manual’s alignment chapter gives a stock camber baseline and explains how to measure with the car at normal ride height. I would record the untouched position and work from there. The 2057 metal-gear servo moves a dual bellcrank with a saver. Slop at the steering wheel can come from saver wear, a loose link or play farther out. If it does not return to center the same way from each direction, check those joints before adding transmitter trim and calling the problem solved.

The manual gives −1 degree front and −1.5 degrees rear as the stock camber references. Those numbers describe an assembled truck at normal ride height. They are useful for restoring a baseline after an impact, not an invitation to tune until the screen on a camber gauge says something more dramatic. Check that the steering centers and the ride height is consistent first. If the pickup points or links are bent, a nominal camber setting can hide the damaged part while leaving the wheel moving through the wrong path.

06 /

Choose a route that gives a rear-drive truck room to finish a turn

The included BFGoodrich replica tread is a reasonable stock choice for firm dirt and mixed short-course practice. Traxxas specifies softer rear tires than fronts and lists axle positions separately in the parts sheet. Preserve those positions when taking the truck apart; the appearance of the wheel face is a weak fitment test. The lower chassis is useful when the surface lets you carry momentum through a bend. On uneven grass, repeated tall roots or deep sand, the ability to maintain that line and cool the motor can disappear before the extra power is useful.

For the first few sessions I would put a wide turn and a straight braking zone on a permitted patch of packed dirt. Coast or brake before the turn, release the brake as the truck settles, and feed the rear tires only as much throttle as the exit accepts. If the entire practice route is hard braking and reaccelerating at walking distance, look for another spot. The manual points out that repeated starts and stops can heat the motor without giving its integrated fan the same cooling opportunity as continuous running.

The shape of the body is useful when you want the visual motion of a short-course truck with covered wheels. It also hangs lower around the tires than the open look of many stadium trucks. Think about whether the ground is clipping the bumpers or throwing the truck on its side when you change direction. The body and tire package work as a pair; changing wheel diameter or offset to gain clearance can bring the tire into the fender on compression. Test fit at the intended ride height before buying an unfamiliar wheel package.

07 /

Look at the hidden access points after running dirt

The clipless shell lifts clear once the body latches are released. The battery has its own latching strap, so neither change calls for loose body clips. Check each latch seats at the end of the battery swap; a body lying flat is not the same as a body positively engaged. The two-piece process also makes it easy to find dirt trapped around the strap or near the body mounts. With the pack disconnected, inspect the cooling paths and the latch faces before putting the truck away.

Traxxas shows an access plug at the gear cover for the slipper and a fixed gear adapter at the motor mount. Consult the manual if a run ends with a whine and poor drive: a slipping clutch and damaged gear teeth are different faults. The manual also specifies cleaning and lubricating the motor periodically, and checking the receiver box seal if you open it. The service sheet is more useful than assuming every 2WD Slash body, front wheel or suspension arm swaps across versions.

The factory documents also separate the front and rear tire assemblies. Write down which pair came off each end before starting a wheel or suspension inspection. On a rear-drive truck, a rear tire that is visibly more worn than its partner can affect the next comparison even if the steering trim never moved. Reassemble the documented front and rear positions, then change only the setting you meant to test. Between the servo saver, the GTR collars and the rear slipper, the VX3 already offers several places to make a change without guessing.

I would leave a second battery, optional gearing and telemetry accessories out of the first total until I know whether the regular driving spot justifies any of them.

09 /

Pick the short-course configuration your route calls for

The Slash 2WD XL-5 58234-8 is the straightforward comparison. Its brushed system, included NiMH and charger make for a simpler purchase if your goal is learning on a short track or buying for a class that specifies the Titan and XL-5. Confirm the rules in either case. A factory VX3 Race profile answers the no-reverse question only, and the club may separately restrict the chassis and electronics.

The ARRMA Senton 223S is the useful comparison if your first complaint about a 2WD truck is having to ease out of every loose turn. It adds a front drive axle and uses a different radio, battery arrangement and service path. The Traxxas Slash Late Model BL-2s, by contrast, suits a prepared oval and the right class. It does not replace a short-course truck simply because both run on dirt. Match the planned course and its rules before comparing motor labels.

10 /

The VX3 makes sense when you have room to drive it

I would shortlist this Slash if the rear-drive layout is a feature for you and you can use the low chassis on firm, open dirt. It combines the adjustable dampers, lower electronics position, radio and rear transmission in a sensible stock package. It costs more to complete than the vehicle line suggests, and its 3S ceiling is not a reason to start with 3S. The most useful first upgrade may be finding a better course rather than buying parts.

Check for the black 58434-4 suffix, the V3200 and VX3 together and a factory transmitter before you count on the reviewed package. Read the exact pack list, plan your first sessions around 2S, and drive the stock truck before committing to setup changes. If you need an included battery, simpler power or a brushed class entry, start with the exact XL-5 package instead.

BEFORE YOU ORDER /

The questions worth asking.

Is model 58434-4 the BL-2s Slash?+

No. This 2026 short-course truck comes with a Velineon V3200 and VX3. Match the model number and installed electronics when you order.

Can I use the battery from the Slash XL-5 bundle?+

The included NiMH pack from the brushed bundle is not compatible with VX3. The 58434-4 manual specifies compatible Traxxas iD 2S or 3S LiPo packs.

Do I need a 3S pack right away?+

No. The manual supports specific 2S packs and 3S packs. A supported 2S pack is a sensible starting point for learning the truck.

Does the stock radio provide steering assistance?+

The TQi Sport system has Traxxas Stability Management. It is a steering aid, separate from the VX3 drive profiles.

Can this truck enter a spec Slash class?+

Only if that event’s rules accept the 58434-4 chassis and VX3 power system. A race profile that removes reverse does not determine eligibility.
