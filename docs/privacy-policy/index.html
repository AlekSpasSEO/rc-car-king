Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated 15 Oct 2026

This policy explains the data involved when you visit RC Car King. The publication is run by Aleksandar Spasevski. It describes the current research-led review website and the external services it uses.

Information involved in a visit

You can read our reviews without an account. RC Car King currently has no registration, newsletter signup, comments, payment form or checkout. Optional Google Analytics runs only after you accept analytics cookies. We store your accept or reject choice in browser local storage for up to 180 days; you can change it using Cookie settings in the footer.

Like other websites, delivering a page involves request information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information and the time of the request. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes request data to deliver the site, detect abuse and maintain its services. Request headers may include a referring page, depending on your browser and the referring site's settings.

Review filters run in your browser. Their selected values can appear in the page URL so you can share the same search. We do not send them to a separate search or analytics service, but a requested URL can be included in hosting logs. Avoid entering personal or sensitive information in a search box or shared URL.

Optional Google Analytics

If you choose Accept analytics, we load Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, traffic sources, page views, scrolling and outbound link use. Google receives browser and device information and request data, and analytics cookies such as _ga can distinguish visits. We do not load the Google Analytics tag or send analytics events before you accept. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from reading the site.

Our implementation keeps advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization consent denied. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled in the website tag. Page-view URLs omit query strings and fragments. Do not put personal or sensitive information in site URLs or search fields.

Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice. Reject analytics stops further measurement through this tag and removes its accessible first-party _ga cookies. Withdrawing consent does not automatically delete information already processed by Google. If your browser blocks local storage, your choice applies only to the current page.

Hosting, fonts and external services

Cloudflare Pages hosts and delivers RC Car King. Depending on the security features in use and the request, Cloudflare may use necessary security cookies or challenges.

Our pages request fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's font servers, which receive information needed to handle the request, such as your IP address and request headers. The site does not embed YouTube players or other social-media widgets.

The services above may process information outside your country. Their processing, retention and transfer arrangements are described in their own privacy information. We do not claim that all service-provider logs are deleted after a particular number of days.

Amazon and other outbound links

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Our Amazon links include an affiliate identifier that lets Amazon attribute a qualifying purchase to this publication. Following one takes you to Amazon, where Amazon's cookies, privacy policy and purchase terms apply. RC Car King does not receive your payment details or your Amazon account credentials.

The price and availability display reads a public offer file from this website. Amazon product checks are performed separately during publication; your browser does not receive our API credentials or contact the Amazon product API to load that file.

Links to manufacturers, independent coverage and Coffeedant take you to separate websites. Their data practices apply when you visit them.

Messages and privacy requests

For a question, correction or privacy request, use the founder's existing contact page at Coffeedant and state that your message concerns RC Car King. That is an external website with its own form and privacy practices, rather than a form hosted on RC Car King.

Information you choose to send, such as your name, reply address, message and relevant page URL, is used to answer your request and keep an appropriate record of the correspondence. Please do not send payment details, passwords or other sensitive information. Correspondence is kept as needed to address the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Your choices and applicable rights

You can manage cookies and external requests through your browser settings and extensions. Blocking external fonts may change the typeface without preventing you from reading the reviews. You can choose not to follow affiliate or other external links.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or a restriction on the use of personal information, or to object to particular processing. Contact the publisher with enough context to identify your request; we may need to verify it before responding. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.

Children and policy updates

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has supplied personal information in correspondence concerning RC Car King, contact the publisher so the situation can be reviewed.
