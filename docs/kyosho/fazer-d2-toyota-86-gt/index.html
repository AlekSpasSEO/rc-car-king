The purpose of the Readyset is to begin with a coordinated baseline, not to imply that no control checks are necessary. Drivetrain and power: Fazer D2 Toyota 86 GT Tuned Lightning Red

Only the rear wheels are driven. The D2's high-mounted motor and drift-specific weight distribution distinguish it from the general Fazer touring arrangement. Kyosho lists a brushed G22 motor, 9.1:1 overall ratio and plastic drift tires. Those establish a coherent casual-drift package. They do not prove a particular throttle feel, wheel speed or advantage over sensored-brushless competition electronics.

The practical appeal of RWD is learning the relationship between rear-wheel traction, throttle and countersteering. It is a different discipline from an AWD touring car sliding with all four wheels powered. An attractive body cannot bridge that difference. If your intended venue expects RWD, this package starts in the correct broad category, but the track's tire and setup requirements still need checking. If it expects a specific motor or controller system, stock brushed equipment becomes a real purchase consideration.

I would use the supplied power system long enough to establish whether the overall package suits the intended practice. An immediate motor, controller, radio and servo replacement can erase the Readyset’s value advantage. The manufacturer’s motor mount supports pinion changes, but that flexibility is not a recommendation to raise gearing before the first drift. Learn a repeatable stock maneuver, monitor the car according to the manual and change one item only after identifying the reason. This review supplies no measured temperature, speed or runtime number for that configuration.

Radio and setup: Fazer D2 Toyota 86 GT Tuned Lightning Red

The KRG-331D’s built-in gyro can moderate both steering and throttle. Kyosho documents separate sensitivity adjustments, so it is important to understand that assistance is not restricted to moving the front wheels. A buyer who wants traditional steering-only gyro behavior should read the D2 instructions carefully and establish which adjustments match the intended driving style. The system is a tool to learn, not evidence that any surface will automatically produce a controlled slide.

The KT-231P+ offers steering/throttle trims and steering dual rate in the documented package. Set neutral positions and check the controller startup procedure before driving. Change only one assistance adjustment between attempts, keeping tire and ground conditions constant. If the car suddenly behaves differently after a tire or surface change, return to the recorded baseline before deciding the gyro is the sole cause.

Steering and suspension: Fazer D2 Toyota 86 GT Tuned Lightning Red

The drift-specific chassis incorporates long suspension arms and a steering arrangement intended for substantial countersteer. Kyosho identifies a dedicated servo working with the gyro. Those are meaningful package facts for a first RWD drifter. They are not permission to print an unverified maximum steering-angle number or guarantee that the car will recover every large slide. Mechanical range, endpoints, tire contact and assistance need to work together.

With the car powered according to its instructions, sweep the steering gently and watch for a wheel or linkage meeting a stop while the servo continues to push. Use the documented transmitter settings and avoid treating maximum travel as the only desirable setup. A tire touching the body or a linkage binding at the end of movement should be investigated physically, not hidden under a bigger steering command.

The chassis has a 260 mm stock wheelbase and a documented 240–274 mm adjustment range. Changing wheelbase for another shell is a mechanical configuration project involving body fit and clearances; it is not a reason to replace the finished Toyota86 immediately. Keep the standard geometry for initial practice and make notes when changing alignment. We assess the documented adjustment possibilities as ownership tools. There is no measured claim here that one wheelbase or geometry setting will give your track more grip or make tandem running easier.

Where to drive: Fazer D2 Toyota 86 GT Tuned Lightning Red

This is a smooth-ground drift car. The plastic drift tires and approximately 12 mm chassis height explain why grass, gravel and jumps are outside the sensible purchase brief. Find clean pavement or a suitable indoor drift surface with room for repeated circles. Loose grit and raised edges can interrupt a slide abruptly. The floor is part of the setup, not merely the place where the car happens to sit.

If you plan to visit a drift club, confirm the required tire before buying extra sets. A venue may standardize grip and pace with a prescribed tire. The supplied plastic tire is a starting configuration, not proof of eligibility. Ask whether staff or local drivers can help with this platform and its assistance system. That support can be more useful to a newcomer than another advertised geometry option that has no clear setup baseline.

Start with a repeatable circle in each direction, then link simple turns without altering multiple settings. Keep the ground clean and observe whether a change in dust or temperature affects consistency before buying hardware. After the session inspect tires, steering movement and debris around the electronics tray. The Readyset is intended to simplify entry, but control practice and maintenance still form part of ownership. We have not established a whole-car water-use claim here; a dry predictable surface is also the more useful environment for learning drift behavior.

That is a genuine convenience for the buyer who wants a handsome car without trimming and painting a shell. Light buckets do not mean LEDs are included. If illuminated detail appeals, check the model’s supported light equipment and its separate cost. Buying verdict: Fazer D2 Toyota 86 GT Tuned Lightning Red

The Toyota86 D2 earns a recommendation for a casual first RWD drifter who wants the body and basic electronics coordinated. The stock G22 motor, dedicated servo, receiver/gyro and finished hidden-mount shell form an understandable purchase. Its reservations are separate power equipment, venue-dependent tires and the possibility that a buyer already committed to another electronics system would immediately replace much of the package they paid for.

Our five editorial categories assess entry completeness, drift-specific equipment, documented adjustment, service planning and complete-purchase value for that buyer. No measured slide consistency, steering speed, top speed, impact resistance or reliability result is assigned. The independent release coverage is clearly labeled as announcement context and its regional suffix is retained. The manufacturer establishes the mechanism; the API establishes the exact T1 listing and offer.

I would choose the car when you want to spend the first sessions learning the stock controls on smooth ground, with the red Toyota body already finished. Confirm battery compatibility, note the baseline assistance settings and ask the venue about tires before spending on options. I would skip it for rough-ground use or an upgrade plan that already removes its main stock equipment. For the named beginner, the D2 is a coherent way to start a drifting project, provided the practice floor and complete budget receive the same attention as the attractive GT86 shell.
