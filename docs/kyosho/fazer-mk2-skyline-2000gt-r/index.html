Drivetrain and power: Fazer Mk2 Nissan Skyline 2000GT-R KPGC10 Silver

The Skyline uses shaft-driven full-time 4WD and gear differentials front and rear. The G14L is brushed, and Kyosho publishes a 6.54:1 standard ratio for the platform. Full ball bearings reduce the need to decide on a bearing purchase during initial completion. Those are real equipment facts; they should not be extended into a quantified efficiency, grip or top-speed claim without an actual test.

The differentials allow each axle’s wheels to turn at different speeds in corners, while the shaft links front and rear drive. This is a conventional touring arrangement rather than a rear-drive drift system. A buyer attracted to the tuned overfenders may reasonably imagine a drift car, but the standard VTC-tire Readyset is sold with a road-driving brief. Decide which discipline you want before treating the body’s visual attitude as a mechanical specification.

The motor mount provides documented positions for different pinions. That can make later gearing work easier to understand, but it does not make every taller ratio appropriate to the supplied motor or your intended pack. Establish the installed setup, check free wheel rotation and listen for an unexpected mechanical problem during gentle running. More power cannot cure an incorrectly seated shaft or debris around a joint. I would keep the supplied brushed system for the first ownership stage because this review’s value case is the complete stock Skyline, not a shell acquired as the beginning of a costly electronics rebuild.

Radio and setup: Fazer Mk2 Nissan Skyline 2000GT-R KPGC10 Silver

The supplied transmitter gives a pistol-grip driving interface with documented trim and steering adjustment. Set neutral positions and verify steering direction before running. The basic setup task is shorter than installing your own radio in a kit, but still important. Check the actual speed controller's calibration and startup instructions rather than assuming a familiar-looking Kyosho radio guarantees identical settings across every regional car.

Steering and suspension: Fazer Mk2 Nissan Skyline 2000GT-R KPGC10 Silver

Oil-filled shocks and independent suspension form a useful stock equipment package for a casual road driver. The KS202W steering servo is fitted, so there is no need to choose a servo merely to get a complete car running. The stock geometry and wheel offsets still need respect if you change wheels later. A different rim can look suitable while moving the tire into the body at full steering or suspension travel.

The manufacturer’s wheelbase for the standard FZ02 Skyline is 260 mm. The stock car's stance is coordinated with its body rather than left for the buyer to establish from a generic chassis kit. Check the exact model instructions before using longer-platform parts or another shell. The broader Fazer family supports several body lengths; that flexibility does not make all mounting arrangements interchangeable without work.

For adjustment, preserve the stock baseline and alter one thing on a familiar surface. If changing shock settings, compare left and right movement and avoid confusing a sticking pivot with damping. The decorative brake discs and calipers are scale details, not separate functional brakes that need a tuning procedure. Our rating credits the understandable supplied suspension and documented platform, without claiming a measured steering improvement over another Fazer. For this buyer, the practical value is learning a complete standard car before deciding whether there is a specific adjustment worth making.

Where to drive: Fazer Mk2 Nissan Skyline 2000GT-R KPGC10 Silver

The silver Skyline is a low road model. I would use clean pavement or a suitable smooth RC track area, with room to turn and brake away from hard edges. The VTC tires and scale body are part of that brief. A dusty parking lot may be discussed in the manufacturer’s sealed-transmission claims, but dust is not the same thing as large loose stones, deep grass or off-road obstacles.

Begin with gentle straight runs and broad turns to verify the controls and body clearance. Keep a predictable route and pay attention to debris that can abruptly interrupt a line. This is particularly helpful for someone returning to RC after years away, since the finished appearance can make the model feel more familiar than the actual radio and battery setup. A calm first session confirms your own car rather than attempting to reproduce a quick action-video sequence.

After running, remove loose dirt around the wheels, shocks and shafts and check that nothing has wrapped around the driveline. Inspect the body mounting and mirrors before putting the car away. The servo’s waterproof designation does not establish unlimited wet use for every connector and moving component. Follow the complete maintenance guidance and actual electronics limits. We have not tested water exposure or long-term wear. The recommendation is for dry scale pavement driving, where the exact body and stock running equipment provide the ownership experience the buyer selected.

Buying verdict: Fazer Mk2 Nissan Skyline 2000GT-R KPGC10 Silver

The silver Skyline’s case is straightforward: a finished Hakosuka subject with coordinated radio, brushed power, 4WD, bearings and oil shocks. It is attractive for the vintage Nissan buyer who wants to begin with road driving rather than kit completion. Its reservations are separate battery equipment, a limited smooth-ground role and the risk of buying it mainly to replace its stock system before use.

Our five categories assess Readyset completeness, subject presentation, documented running gear, service planning and whole-purchase value for the scale pavement buyer. No measured cornering, speed, crash survival or reliability score is assigned. The earlier T1 video coverage is labeled as context and its manufacturer footage remains distinct from independent testing. Current C-suffix documentation and API identity establish the exact reviewed offer rather than an assumed family-wide equivalence.

I would choose this Skyline when the silver body is the object you want and the standard brushed road package suits your driving area. Arrange the pack and charger, establish the neutral controls and enjoy a few stock sessions before considering options. I would skip it for grass, a dedicated RWD drift track or a conversion plan that makes most included equipment redundant. On the intended terms, it is a clear complete-car purchase whose appearance and ownership convenience support each other, provided the buyer respects the low road chassis beneath the memorable shell.
