01 /

The body matters, but the N-RM label matters more

The strongest reason to shortlist this Mini-Z is simple: you want a finished Porsche 911 GT3 RS that can drive on a suitable small-car surface, and you want to learn a recognizable hobby platform beneath it. The body is part of the appeal rather than an interchangeable decoration in the buying calculation. That does not mean every chassis sold under the MR-04 name has the same layout, equipment or setup requirements.

Kyosho identifies 32358OR as MR-04N-RM: narrow configuration, rear motor and a 90mm wheelbase. The car is listed at 168mm long and 72mm wide. Those figures make this a particular short-wheelbase Porsche, rather than the wider 98mm mid-motor car described by some copied retailer text. A setup from a different MR-04 can be a research lead; it is not automatically an instruction for this one.

The outside evidence here is supporting context, not a borrowed driving test. RC Team’s MR-04 overview discusses the platform changes, while an exact-model specialist listing identifies the Porsche’s N-RM arrangement. Where retailer dimensions disagree, the exact Kyosho product page takes priority. We have not driven this Porsche, compared lap times or measured how its rear-motor balance behaves on any particular track.

02 /

Finished car, matched radio, eight cells to add

The Readyset supplies the assembled chassis with the painted and decaled Lava Orange Porsche body, installed motor and electronics, wheels and tires, and a KT-531P transmitter. It also includes front-tread adjustment parts, spare nuts, a wheel wrench, a pinion tool and 6T, 7T, 8T and 9T pinions. Supplied tuning parts are not a direction to install the highest gearing before learning the stock arrangement.

The remaining basic power requirement is four AAA cells in the vehicle and four AAA cells in the transmitter. That creates a different starting basket from a micro car supplied with an integrated rechargeable battery, or a larger RTR that needs an external LiPo system. With the alkaline example used here, there is no separate traction charger to buy. Cells are consumables, so a low initial accessories bill does not establish a low lifetime running cost.

The documentation needs careful reading on rechargeable chemistry. Equipment blocks in the product material are not perfectly consistent about where NiMH is permitted, and the English manual’s chassis completion illustration specifies alkaline AAA cells. Our basic budget therefore uses eight alkaline AAA cells, an unambiguous starting basis. If choosing a rechargeable arrangement, confirm the applicable instructions for each compartment instead of treating AAA shape alone as electrical approval.

The radio is part of the matched package. KT-531P uses the stated FHSS system; Kyosho distinguishes it from ASF, MHS and FHS compatibility. Similar branding or a transmitter that works with another Mini-Z is not sufficient evidence that it will bind to this Readyset. A buyer with existing radio equipment should verify the exact protocol before valuing the included handset as redundant.

03 /

A brushed rear-motor car, not a miniature EVO2

The installed motor is a 130-class brushed unit driving the rear wheels through a gear differential. This is the ready-to-run RWD platform, not a brushless EVO2 electronics package. Brushed operation, its supplied radio and its battery format should be evaluated together. An attractive MR-04 racing upgrade article does not convert the stock car into the configuration shown in that article, and a speculative conversion cost is not part of this review’s value score.

The rear-motor designation describes where the motor sits relative to the rear axle assembly. Together with the short 90mm wheelbase, it identifies which chassis references and body arrangements are relevant. It gives a reason to check matching setup notes, but not enough evidence to declare this Porsche inherently faster or easier than a longer mid-motor car. Tire choice, surface, gearing, control settings and driver input still affect the result.

Full ball bearings are documented stock equipment, so there is no reason to budget a speculative mandatory bearing conversion. The gear differential also belongs in the baseline specification. Neither feature removes the need to keep grit away from a small drivetrain. Inspect the actual installed pinion and its mesh before changing gearing, retain the original parts, and follow the manual’s assembly arrangement rather than selecting a ratio solely because a larger pinion is in the bag.

04 /

Training mode is the first control worth preserving

Kyosho supplies this Readyset in training mode. Treat that as a useful initial operating state and consult the exact manual before changing it. It is separate from steering rate, which changes the steering response range available at the transmitter, and separate from the trims used to establish neutral. A new owner should understand which control changed before interpreting a different response as a mechanical fault.

The KT-531P provides steering trim, throttle trim and steering-rate adjustment. Before the first floor run, check that the wheels are clear, the vehicle responds in the intended directions and neutral is correct. The manual’s sequence switches on the transmitter before the chassis. On a small car the controls can appear deceptively toy-like, but accidental drive or steering movement can still send the body into nearby furniture or track barriers.

The optional gyro is MZW711 according to the current exact product page. It is not included in the stock purchase, and an older accessory code on a retailer listing should not override that reference. A gyro is also not a replacement for correct trim, free steering movement or an appropriate tire and surface combination. Learn the supplied configuration before deciding that an additional control system solves a demonstrated problem.

05 /

Know what the MR-04 changes actually include

The MR-04 front arrangement uses revised knuckles with integrated kingpins, and the upper-arm movement changes camber through suspension travel. Kyosho describes reduced scrub radius relative to the previous platform. These are documented design changes, not measurements made by this publication. They also do not mean the Readyset supplies every adjustment, spring or damping part used in a developed club racing setup.

The published front and rear tracks are 59.5mm and 59.0mm respectively. The front wheels use a narrow format with a listed +1.5mm offset, while the rear wheels are wide with 0.0mm offset. Those numbers are different from the 72mm overall body width. Confusing track, offset and body width makes it easy to order a wheel that physically mounts but does not sit correctly beneath this specific Porsche shell.

At the rear, the motor and axle assembly works with the chassis through the T-bar arrangement, and the stock specification includes a coil-spring damper. A rear oil shock is optional; it should not appear in the stock pros or completed purchase price as though already supplied. Check the actual rear assembly for free movement before following another driver’s suspension recommendation, particularly when that recommendation uses a different motor mount or wheelbase.

06 /

Choose the clean surface before the car

This Porsche makes the most sense on a clean, smooth indoor road-car venue or a Mini-Z track whose surface and rules accept the stock configuration. Ask about the local class before buying for organized use: a complete Readyset is not proof of eligibility for every event. Rules may distinguish chassis, motor, body, batteries and tires. A compatible local class can be more useful than an impressive modification list from another venue.

The low road-car body and small wheels are poor reasons to choose lawn, gravel or broken paving. At this scale, debris and abrupt joints are meaningful obstacles. A clean floor is not automatically suitable either: dust, polished finishes, furniture and people can make a short route inconsistent or unsafe. Set out a clear practice area and verify permission to use it instead of assuming an indoor-size car can run anywhere indoors.

The street-car theme does not make 32358OR an AWD drift package. Rear-wheel drive and a Porsche shell do not supply dedicated drift tires or a documented drift setup. If sustained indoor drifting is the main objective, compare a complete vehicle configured for that job. Here, sensible route choice means giving the supplied road-car equipment a useful task rather than changing its purpose through the title of a video.

07 /

Preserve the body fit that makes this the Porsche version

The finished GT3 RS shell is a substantive part of this purchase. Its lights, rear wing and recognizable profile are why many buyers will choose it over another body on the platform. Manufacturer photographs show the actual model, but lighting can make Lava Orange look redder on a screen. The useful identifiers remain the 32358OR code and official color name, rather than a promise that every display reproduces the finish identically.

The manual shows the body engaging at the nose mount and then seating through the side mounting arrangement. Release and refit it as illustrated; do not treat the rear wing as a carrying handle or pull the shell upward as if it were held by conventional external body posts. Proper seating matters before a run, because a loose or incorrectly located shell changes clearance around the wheels and exposes decorative parts to avoidable contact.

For storage, the manual directs removing the vehicle batteries after use rather than relying indefinitely on the power switch. Small cells remain electrical components, and the chassis is not a display stand that can be forgotten with batteries installed. Keep the matching wheel wrench and pinion tool with the car, label removed setup parts and retain the exact manual. That organization is particularly useful when several similar Mini-Z bodies and wheel configurations share a collection.

09 /

Compare the task, then the package

A different complete MR-04 body configuration can make sense if your local setup references and class fit that layout better. Confirm its exact wheelbase, motor position and body width instead of assuming a body change alone reproduces it. The MINI-Z SC430 in our archive is a useful full-review comparison for that configuration question. Its chassis label should be checked in its own review, rather than carried into this Porsche’s specification table.

The MINI-Z AWD Nissan Skyline alternative serves a different starting aim, particularly for a buyer interested in the supplied AWD drift-oriented package. Compare its exact tires, drivetrain and radio requirements before deciding. The Porsche’s road-car identity is attractive, but it does not make it the economical answer to every scale-car discipline. Buy the version whose documented stock equipment serves the driving you actually want to practice.

The Team Associated RC28T Zoom is the simpler casual miniature comparison, with an integrated rechargeable battery and a much lower dated vehicle reference. It is not a substitute for this Porsche’s chassis ecosystem or licensed body. It belongs in the decision when the actual requirement is a small hard-floor recreational car rather than a specific Mini-Z. Keep expectations and the complete power package visible on both sides of that comparison.

10 /

A purposeful Porsche rather than a generic MR-04 bargain

I would shortlist 32358OR when the Porsche body and its 90mm rear-motor format are the point of the purchase. The finished appearance, documented bearings and differential, matched radio and training mode provide a credible stock package. That judgment is about equipment and buyer fit. It is not a claim of superior lap times, measured grip or durability against a different Mini-Z.

I would pass if the available venue is unsuitable, the target class needs another configuration, or the buyer primarily wants an AWD drift car. Verify N-RM, 90mm and 32358OR at purchase, add the eight-cell power requirement and keep the initial setup restrained. This is a satisfying specification-led proposition when its exact identity helps the decision, rather than being a detail discovered after ordering incompatible setup parts.

BEFORE YOU ORDER /

The questions worth asking.

Is this Porsche a 98mm mid-motor MR-04?+

No. The exact manufacturer page identifies 32358OR as narrow MR-04N-RM with a 90mm wheelbase. Conflicting retailer copy should not override it.

Is the motor brushless?+

No. This complete RWD Readyset uses the specified 130-class brushed motor; it is not an EVO2 brushless package.

How many cells do I need?+

Four AAA cells for the car and four AAA cells for the transmitter, all separate. The basic budget uses alkaline cells; verify each compartment’s instructions before choosing a rechargeable arrangement.

Does it include a gyro or rear oil shock?+

Neither is stock. The exact page identifies MZW711 as the optional gyro; rear oil damping is also optional.

Can I use an older Mini-Z radio?+

Do not assume compatibility. KT-531P uses the stated FHSS system, distinguished from ASF, MHS and FHS in the manufacturer material.

Is the USD 201.99 total a current Amazon price?+

No. It combines a dated USD 189.99 exact-model retailer reference and a labeled USD 12 alkaline-cell allowance, before tax and shipping.
