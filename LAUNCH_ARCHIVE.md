# RC Car King launch archive

Published 13 October 2026: **50 complete-vehicle reviews across 8 brands**, including the original Nissan 240SX review. The launch contains 111,493 words of core article analysis; each review has at least 2,000 body-paragraph words, plus the product box, tables, FAQs and evidence record.

These are research-led buying assessments. Ratings are the mean of five disclosed category judgments for the named buyer; physical tests and personal ownership are not claimed. Every car has an exact Amazon Creators API identity match and credited manufacturer images or technical illustrations. Static price references are independently sourced manufacturer/retailer references; current Amazon offers are timestamped and expire after 24 hours.

The owner authorized this initial 50-review launch as an exception to the daily cadence. All launch dates reflect the actual launch day. The existing one-review-per-day schedule resumes after the launch. rccarking.com remains unconnected.
