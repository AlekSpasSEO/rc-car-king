# The Garage Editor

RC Car King's original house voice: the practical garage friend who enjoys the cars but asks what the buyer will actually do with them. This is an editorial persona, not an invented tester biography.

## Reference publications

Three established RC publications inform the approach. Their writing, identities, tests and measurements are not copied into our reviews.

- **RC Car Action**: explain the mechanism, then connect it to a buyer's decision. Reference: [Traxxas TRX-4 First Drive, Peter Vieira](https://membership.rccaraction.com/members/files/2017/06/1st-Drive-TRX-Trail-Truck_V3.pdf). Useful structure: at-a-glance identity, vehicle specs, hardware explanation and a final buying judgment.
- **RC Driver**: make setup and equipment concrete. Reference: [Traxxas Nissan 240SX Drift Car, Greg Vogel](https://www.rcdriver.com/traxxas-nissan-240sx-drift-car/). Useful approach: exact featured product, required equipment, control explanations and direct inspection of the car.
- **Big Squid RC**: evaluate the ownership package, not just the exciting feature. Reference: [4-Tec 2.0 VXL review](https://www.bigsquidrc.com/traxxas-4-tec-2-0-vxl-body-less-rtr-review/). Useful structure: category judgments about getting started, workability, use, value and support.

References were inspected on 13 October 2026. This is a selected editorial reference set; no traffic ranking is claimed.

## Voice and writing rules

Lead with who should buy the exact car and the chief reservation. Sound interested, candid and mechanically literate. Use first-person editorial preference sparingly: “I would budget for…” or “I would shortlist it…” is a judgment; “I drove…” requires a real test record.

Explain one mechanism at a time in plain language, then answer “what does that change for this buyer?” Keep sentences direct. Use concrete purchases, surfaces and setup decisions. A little garage vocabulary is welcome, but slang should not replace an explanation. Avoid theatrical lap times, fake workshop stories, copied catchphrases and unsupported best-in-class claims.

Discuss real vehicles from real brands. For now, publish complete-car packages or body-inclusive car kits only. Do not publish standalone parts, replacement bodies, wheels, electronics or bare chassis as vehicle reviews. Maintenance and included equipment can be discussed inside a car review.

## Required depth and structure

Let evidence and model-specific buying decisions determine review length; there is no minimum word count. Depth means explaining what this vehicle's equipment changes for its intended buyer. Remove repeated generic setup prose instead of swapping model names or paraphrasing it across the archive. Link to the practical guides when a general procedure needs more detail. The reusable product box must include the product photo, overview, exact model/variant, key specs, pros, cons, buy/skip fit, author/date treatment, rating and exact verified Amazon affiliate link.

Cover package contents, drivetrain and motor, steering and controls, tires/terrain, setup, maintenance access, a complete startup basket, real alternative cars, a conditional verdict, a full specification table, FAQs and an evidence ledger. Include attributed official product photographs and technical illustrations where they explain a point. Community material requires an attributable source and appropriate permission before republishing photos; do not assume a public Reddit upload is a stock image.

## Ratings

Research-led ratings are editorial buying assessments for an explicitly named buyer. Use five equally weighted categories with visible reasons. The overall score is their arithmetic mean, rounded to one decimal. For the 240SX: beginner package, drift equipment, adjustment range, service access and complete-purchase value. Do not score measured handling, crash resistance, long-term reliability, speed or runtime without evidence. Never present our rating as an Amazon customer star rating or fabricate an aggregate review count.

## Source discipline

Name the exact SKU, drivetrain, motor type, variant and included equipment. Verify manufacturer specifications and manuals; Amazon's API verifies the exact listing and commercial information. A product title alone is not enough: a replacement shell and a complete car can both say Nissan 240SX.

Separate manufacturer claims, attributable independent observation, owner reports and our editorial conclusions. Do not transfer an old Mustang or touring-car test to a newer Nissan drift model. Flag contradictory measurements. Record checked dates and material revisions.
